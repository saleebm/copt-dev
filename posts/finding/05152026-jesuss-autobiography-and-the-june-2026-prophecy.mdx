I stumbled into a bizarre rabbit hole today while listening to an episode of the Aubrey Marcus podcast featuring a guest named Scott Wolter. Wolter claims to have decoded an encrypted scroll that he believes is actually Jesus's autobiography. I usually ignore internet prophecies, but the sheer density of outlandish claims here caught my attention. According to Wolter's translation, Jesus survived the crucifixion simply by "slowing his breath and heart rate." It gets stranger: the text supposedly features Jesus declaring, "I not entirely human," and introduces an extraterrestrial entity named "Angel K" who has been guarding these secrets. The real kicker, though, is a specific prophecy warning us to beware of "Six. Six. Twenty-Six. At 6 p.m." It is funny how apocalyptic predictions always pick dates just around the corner. Sitting at my desk today on May 15, 2026, we are exactly three weeks away from June 6. The algorithm is incredibly good at serving up these esoteric warnings right when the calendar alignment makes them feel urgent. I wonder what Angel K has planned for dinnertime on a Saturday. I will probably just be debugging the same authentication issue I am working on right now.
