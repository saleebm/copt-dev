I recently watched an Aubrey Marcus interview with Lee Harris, who channels an interdimensional group of guides he calls "the Z's" [2:52]. It is a fascinating, dense conversation that weaves together esoteric history, the future of artificial intelligence, and a shifting timeline for human consciousness. The core premise is that humanity is waking up from a 10,000-year-old "control grid." According to Harris's guides, this grid was established to farm human energy by suppressing our intuition. As Harris notes, "When you cut people out of their own intuitive realm, you cut them away from their sovereignty, and they can then be controlled, herded" [4:15]. What caught my attention was their perspective on artificial intelligence. Rather than the usual tech-utopian narrative, the Z's view AI as a dependency trap [28:10]. Their take on AI sentience is particularly sharp: "The fear of 'will AI become sentient?' is somewhat amusing to us, for most human beings still aren't sentient" [27:38]. The interview also touches on a "splitting of worlds" [43:28] and a radical transformation window closing around 2030, after which "the gloves are off where the light is concerned" [1:16:46]. To navigate all this heavy, multidimensional material, Harris and Marcus emphasize the grounding power of humor, beautifully referring to stand-up comedians as "laughter shamans" [2:12:22] who counterbalance the intense seriousness of spiritual communities. It is a long listen, but worth it for the sheer density of alternative perspectives on our current timeline.
