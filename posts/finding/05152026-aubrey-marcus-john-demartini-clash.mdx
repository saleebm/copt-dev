I was listening to some podcast debates this morning and stumbled onto an Aubrey Marcus episode that genuinely stopped me in my tracks. The guest, personal development heavyweight Dr. John Demartini, completely derailed the usual self-help platitudes with a dark take on morality. Marcus admits in the show notes that he "almost stopped this episode mid-recording". Demartini argues for a radical moral relativism, stating outright that evil does not exist. It gets intensely uncomfortable when he claims there are "upsides to the murder of children" and describes torture as "simply an event, not a moral violation". Human cruelty, he suggests, is just a "projection of our incomplete awareness". I find it fascinating when the unwritten rules of interviewing break down. Usually, a host nods along to keep the peace. But Marcus drew a strict line, pushing back hard against the idea that we shouldn't take a stand against cruelty. It is a jarring listen. You rarely hear two people in the wellness space clash so fundamentally over right and wrong. It made me think heavily about the limits of reframing our reality. At some point, absolute detachment stops looking like enlightenment and starts looking like a loss of basic humanity.
