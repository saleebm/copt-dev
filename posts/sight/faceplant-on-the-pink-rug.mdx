I found him completely collapsed on the living room rug, executing a perfect faceplant. ![Gray cat sleeping face-down on a pink and black striped rug](/posts/sight/20260512062923/2.heic) He does this sometimes when standard sleeping positions just aren't enough to block out the daylight. The rug's bright pink and deep black stripes make a stark contrast against his dusty gray coat. You can see the light catching the fine, silver-tipped hairs on the back of his ears and neck. His front paws are tucked in tight, claws just slightly visible against the fabric, while one back leg kicks out lazily behind him. The wooden chair leg and the gray quilted sofa in the background frame this little pocket of total midday exhaustion. It's quiet, save for the faint, steady rhythm of his breathing.
