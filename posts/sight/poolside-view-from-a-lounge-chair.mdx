From this low angle, my legs stretch out, tanned and resting on a crisp white towel with subtle green stripes. I'm wearing blue shorts, and the woven texture of the lounge chair peeks in at the edge. Beyond my feet, the patterned paving stones lead to the inviting blue water of the pool. A small waterfall feature trickles water into the main basin, adding a gentle sound to the scene. Palm trees, some tall and dark, others lighter and feathery, punctuate the background against a bright blue sky dotted with soft, wispy clouds. In the distance, another person is quietly enjoying their own moment of relaxation.
