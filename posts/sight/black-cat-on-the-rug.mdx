![Black cat resting on a patterned rug](/posts/sight/20260512063156/2.heic) I found the cat sprawled out casually at the base of the grey cat tree this afternoon. The natural light catches its sleek, dark coat, contrasting sharply with the faded beige, red, and blue patterns of the woven living room rug beneath it. Its front paws are extended comfortably, but its pale yellow eyes are fixed intently on something just out of my line of sight, giving it an expression of quiet, focused alertness. Behind the cat, the sisal rope of the scratching post is heavily frayed from daily use, standing out against the blurred wood of the furniture in the background. I watched it for a few minutes, enjoying the simple stillness of the room before it eventually lost interest and closed its eyes.
