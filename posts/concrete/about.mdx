# What this is

This site is a notebook left open on the table. Mostly it's me thinking out loud about how to build software without losing the thread of being a person. If you find one thing here that helps you ship something — or stop shipping something — it did its job.

I'm Mina. I write code, mostly TypeScript, mostly at night, and I run a lot of agents in parallel so I can keep the boring parts of the loop moving while I think about the interesting parts. If any of that sounds familiar, you're in the right place.

## Two stories I keep coming back to

> "Call me Ishmael." — Melville, *Moby-Dick*, ch. 1

Every builder is chasing something pale and enormous past the shipping lanes. The useful question isn't *will I catch it.* It's *who do I become while chasing it, and do I take the crew down with me.* If you are reading this in the middle of a chase, that's the question I'd lay on your desk.

> "We said there warn't no home like a raft, after all." — Twain, *Huckleberry Finn*, ch. 18

The river is also an option. Lashed-together commits, leaks a little, gets you there. Not every project needs to be a whaler. Some need to be a raft. Knowing which one you're on this week is half the work.
