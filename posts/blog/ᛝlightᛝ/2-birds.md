# 2 Birds

Getting out of my own way.

## The dead bird and the live bird

What I have no longer in my conception - I no longer consciously or subconsciously value. What I no longer value, I no longer do. What I no longer do, I no longer am. I am alive only to the extent that I value and do.

I've shed the skin and bathed in the blood of the dead bird. The live bird is the one that I am consciously aware of and subconsciously value. I have brought these to the High Priest \{self|the I am\} to bring about a healing of the dis-ease of the body, mind, and soul.
